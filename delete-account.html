Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Cindy Career Copilot account and everything stored for it on our servers:

  • your profile, résumé and cover letter
  • your job pipeline, activity and chats with Cindy
  • certificates, licenses and saved application answers

This can't be undone. If you want a copy first, sign in and use Profile → Download backup.

You can also do this inside the app: Profile → Delete my account.

Temporary copies made for the auto-apply helper are erased automatically within 24 hours. Forgot your password? Open an issue at our GitHub page without posting private details and we'll help.
